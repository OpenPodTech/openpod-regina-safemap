    ❄ Winter Info: Snow Routes

    When snow routes are declared, no parking for 24 hours on blue-snowflake-signed streets. $120 ticket if you don't move.

    Priority 1 — Major Roads

    Albert St, Victoria Ave, Ring Road, Lewvan Dr, Pasqua St — cleared FIRST within 6 hours of declaration

    Priority 2 — Collector Roads

    Bus routes, school zones, emergency access roads — cleared within 12 hours

    Priority 3 — Residential

    Only plowed after 15cm+ accumulation. Snow pushed to curb line, not removed.

    💡 Sign up for snow route notifications at Regina.ca/SnowRoutes
